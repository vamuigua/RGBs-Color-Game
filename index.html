How to play

You are shown an RGB value. Find the tile that is that color.

The color itself stays hidden while you guess — tap PEEK to flash it for half a second, at the cost of 50% of that round’s points.

Modes

  • ARCADE — 3 lives and a countdown that tightens as you level up.
  • STEADY — same lives, no clock. Take your time.
  • ZEN — no lives, no clock. Keep guessing; wrong guesses only cost points.

Difficulty

  • EASY — 3 tiles, obviously different colors, lightness cues allowed.
  • MEDIUM — 6 tiles, noticeably closer colors.
  • HARD — 9 tiles, colors separated mostly by hue rather than brightness.

Every level narrows the gap between the colors. Get one wrong and it widens again, so the game settles at whatever you can actually see.

Scoring

100 points base, multiplied by difficulty (×1 / ×1.5 / ×2) and by your streak (up to ×5), plus a time bonus in ARCADE.

Keyboard

1–9 pick a tile · arrows move · Enter select · P peek · N new colors · M mute · Esc pause
